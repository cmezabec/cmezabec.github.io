Cristian Meza

Full Professor · Instituto de Ingeniería Matemática (INGEMAT) — CIMFAV
Facultad de Ingeniería, Universidad de Valparaíso

I am a Full Professor at the Mathematical Engineering Institute (INGEMAT), Faculty of Engineering, Universidad de Valparaíso, and a member of the research centre CIMFAV.

I obtained my PhD in Statistics in 2006 from Université Paris-Sud (Orsay), under the supervision of Marc Lavielle (INRIA, École Polytechnique) and Jean-Louis Foulley (INRA, Jouy-en-Josas), with a thesis on extensions and applications of the SAEM algorithm for mixed models.

My research focuses on mixed-effects models (linear, nonlinear and generalized), EM-type and stochastic approximation algorithms (EM, SAEM), REML estimation, MCMC methods, semiparametric and penalized (LASSO-type) models, and segmentation models, with applications in biometrics, biostatistics, microbiome data, epidemiology and astrostatistics.
