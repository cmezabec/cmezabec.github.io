Research interests

  • Mixed-effects models (LME, NLME, GLMM)
  • EM-type algorithms and stochastic approximation (SAEM)
  • REML estimation
  • MCMC and Bayesian computation
  • Semiparametric and penalized (LASSO-type) models
  • Segmentation / change-point models
  • Applications: biometrics, biostatistics, microbiome, epidemiology, astrostatistics
